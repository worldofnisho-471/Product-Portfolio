01 — About

I start from the user problem, ground decisions in evidence, and make sure what ships can be trusted.

At London Stock Exchange Group, I work on the migration of the FxAll institutional FX trading product into Workspace, keeping critical trading workflows and integrations working for users as the platform evolves. Alongside that, I authored Execution Policy Intelligence, an FX product proposal for LSEG’s hackathon.

Outside my day job, I design and build AI products end to end: user research, PRDs, success metrics, and working prototypes, including an app I conceived, built and shipped solo.

Twelve years of quality engineering, including automation frameworks built from scratch, give me a sharp eye for edge cases, risk and release readiness. Those are the same instincts that make a product reliable and trustworthy.

A voice-first AI companion that remembers your story.

AI-powered Research Management Suite, conceived, built and shipped solo.

  • Built to be trusted: real-paper citations, visible assumptions, and a Lead approves every AI change.

FX product proposal · LSEG hackathon

Execution Policy Intelligence

Evidence-backed management of FX execution policies.

  • An Execution Policy Register built on Tradefeedr evidence, tracking each policy’s rationale, evidence and decisions.
  • Design principle: statistics determine, AI explains, a human decides. AI never changes a policy on its own.
  • Covers the competitive landscape, strategic fit, business case and a validate-before-scaling plan.

RAG assistant · HelloPM AI PM course

IITM Knowledge Assistant

Cited answers to IIT Madras student questions, with a refusal to guess.

  • Hybrid BM25 + vector retrieval, reranking 20 candidates down to 5, and a citation on every answer.
  • A grounding gate decides whether to answer, abstain or flag conflicting sources.
  • Eval targets include Recall@5 ≥ 90%, unsupported claims < 1% and 100% pass on prompt-injection tests.
